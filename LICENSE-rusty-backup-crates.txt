Third-party license — disk browser filesystem crates
=====================================================

The front ends' disk browser (iris-gui-core) links these crates from the
rusty-backup project, all published on crates.io under the MIT License:

  sgi-disk 0.1.0, disk-core 0.3.0, part-tables 0.2.0, fs-unix 0.3.0,
  fs-xfs 0.2.0, rb-fsexporter 0.2.0, rb-fsimporter 0.1.0, fs-tar 0.2.0,
  and fs-mac 0.2.0, which they depend on.

  https://github.com/danifunker/rusty-backup
  https://crates.io/crates/sgi-disk

Their own dependencies are under MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD-3-Clause, Zlib,
bzip2-1.0.6 or Unicode-3.0 licenses. IRIS does not link rusty-backup's
application crate.
